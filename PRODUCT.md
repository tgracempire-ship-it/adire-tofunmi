# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Premium Nigerian and diaspora wedding clients, couples, families, and event planners commissioning authentic handwoven Aso Oke for traditional ceremonies, coordinated bridal and groom ensembles, and Aso Ebi groups.

## Product Purpose

Threads of Heritage helps clients discover, configure, estimate, and enquire about bespoke Yoruba Aso Oke directly from master artisan Jesutofunmi. Success means turning cultural confidence and product understanding into a qualified WhatsApp commission request.

## Positioning

An artisan-led digital atelier that combines authentic narrow-loom heritage with practical bespoke planning tools: a loom customizer, curated commission lookbook, wedding packages, and a bulk Aso Ebi estimator.

## Operating Context

Visitors may be planning a traditional wedding locally or from the diaspora, comparing ceremonial looks, coordinating groups, estimating lead times, and preparing a detailed order before contacting the artisan through WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the interactive loom customizer, product filtering and modal, enquiry drawer, currency context, wedding packages, Aso Ebi calculator, heritage videos, testimonials, care guidance, and WhatsApp flows.
- Preserve the established product terminology: Alaari, Sanyan, Etu, Aso Ebi, Gele, Agbada, and handloom.
- Existing React 19, TypeScript, Vite 6, Tailwind CSS v4, and Lucide implementation remains the technical foundation.
- The redesign must work responsively across desktop and mobile and retain accessible semantics and interactions.
- Commercial and production claims already present in the repository are treated as supplied content; no new claims are invented.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Threads of Heritage name, artisan Jesutofunmi, genuine Yoruba weaving context, existing contact details, authentic project photography and video, and a voice that is culturally assured, warm, exacting, and premium without imitation-European royal clichés.

## Evidence on Hand

- Authentic commission and fabric photography in `public/images/`.
- Two loom-weaving videos in `public/images/`.
- Product, wedding package, testimonial, and vendor data in `src/data/` and component source.
- Existing interactive configuration and estimation flows in `src/components/`.

## Product Principles

- Let the cloth and craft demonstrate value before marketing language does.
- Make bespoke commissioning feel understandable and achievable.
- Treat Yoruba terminology and artisan provenance with specificity and respect.
- Serve both local and diaspora planning contexts without flattening the culture.
- Move naturally from inspiration to a detailed, human WhatsApp enquiry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, semantic controls, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, accessible dialogs, and responsive layouts suitable for mobile-first browsing.
